Class Schedule

Our term runs across the week – join any session that fits your timetable.

  • Mondays – 6:00 pm – Beginner Python (2 hours)
  • Wednesdays – 5:00 pm – Web Basics (HTML/CSS) (2 hours)
  • Fridays – 4:00 pm – Data Structures (2 hours)

Each class includes a short lecture, hands‑on exercises, and time for questions. Attendance is free for club members; a nominal fee applies for non‑members.
