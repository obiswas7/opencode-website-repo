About Us

Coding Club is a community‑driven after‑school programme for learners aged 12 and up. Our weekly sessions cover Python, web development, data structures, and more – always in a friendly, low‑pressure environment. No prior experience is needed; just bring a laptop and curiosity.

Our Mission

To inspire the next generation of creators by making programming accessible, practical, and fun. We believe everyone should have the opportunity to learn how to build technology, not just consume it.

Instructors

Our volunteers are passionate developers with industry experience. They donate their time to mentor students and share real‑world insights.
